Stock Management System — User Manual

A step-by-step guide to every module: stock, purchases, payments, expenses, donations, mahaprasad coupons, assets, reports and administration.

Start Here

Getting Started

How to log in, find your way around, and understand what you can and cannot do.

1. Logging in

  1. Open the application (desktop app, or the website link given to you).
  2. Enter your Email Address and Password.
  3. Click Login.
Forgot or want to change your password? After logging in, click your name in the top-right corner → Change Password. See the My Account section.

2. Finding your way around

Every screen has the same layout:

  • Left sidebar — the main menu. Modules are grouped (Purchases, Stock Movement, Inventory, Payments, Expenses, Mahaprasad, Donations, Assets, Reports, Masters, Admin). Click a group to expand it.
  • Top bar — notifications bell and your name/role menu (Profile, Change Password, Logout).
  • Main area — the screen you are working on.
You may not see every menu item. The system shows only the modules your role allows. For example, kitchen staff see Mahaprasad and Stock Out, while trustees also see payment approvals. This is normal — see Roles & Permissions.

3. Things that work the same everywhere

  • Required fields are marked with a red asterisk *. You cannot save until they are filled.
  • Dates default to today. Many forms allow back-dated entries; a few (like donations and mahaprasad) do not allow future dates.
  • Money is always in Rupees (₹).
  • Export buttons (Export Excel / PDF / WhatsApp) appear on most lists and reports.
  • Approval workflow — payments and expenses are first submitted, then a trustee approves them. Until approved they are "Pending".

Module 1

Dashboard

Your home screen — a quick health check of the whole temple store.

Who uses it: Everyone. What you can do: view only (no data is entered here).

What you see

  • Key numbers — Products, Departments, Suppliers, Active Users, and stock warnings (Low Stock, Out of Stock, Reorder Soon, Expiring within 30 days).
  • This Month's Finance — Income, Expenses and Surplus/Deficit, with a View P&L → link.
  • Asset Status — items checked out, overdue, and due this week.
  • Payment Status — payments pending approval, outstanding and overdue amounts.
  • Expiring Within 7 Days, Today's Activity, a 7-Day Stock Movement chart, Top Products, and Recent Transactions.
Every card is a shortcut. Click any number or card to jump straight to that module. The dashboard refreshes itself every few minutes.
Module 2

Purchases

Record goods bought from suppliers. This is how stock comes in.

Who uses it: Store keeper / store manager. Needs permission: create transactions.

Important: There is no separate "Stock In" screen. When you save a Purchase Entry, the system automatically adds the items to stock and creates the supplier invoice.

2.1 Recording a new purchase

  1. Go to Purchases → New Purchase Entry.
  2. Select the Supplier * (the credit term fills the Due Date automatically).
  3. Select the Department * that is receiving the goods.
  4. Optionally enter the Invoice Number and Invoice Date; set the Received Date * (defaults to today).
  5. Under Items Received, for each item choose the Product *, enter Qty *, and optionally Rate (₹), batch reference and Expiry Date.
  6. Click Add Item to add more rows. The Grand Total updates automatically.
  7. Click Save Purchase Entry.
Expiry & batches: For perishable items, always fill the Expiry Date — it powers the "Expiring Stock" report and FIFO (first-in-first-out) issuing.

2.2 Purchase Register (viewing past purchases)

  • Use the tabs All / Unpaid / Partial / Paid and search by entry or invoice number.
  • Click a row to expand and preview its line items, then View full details →.
  • On the detail screen you can Edit (only if no payment has been approved yet) or Void Entry with a reason. Voiding reverses the stock that was added.
Module 3

Stock Movement

Record stock going out, moving between departments, or being wasted.

Who uses it: Store keeper, kitchen staff. Needs permission: create transactions.

3.1 Stock Out (issuing stock)

  1. Set the Date (defaults to today).
  2. Choose the Purpose: Consumption (Kitchen), Distribution (Prasadam), Offering / Puja, or Other.
  3. Select the Product and the From Department. The Current Balance shows; it turns red and says "(Low stock)" if at or below the minimum.
  4. Enter the Quantity (must be more than 0). Optionally fill Issued To and Notes.
  5. Click Record Stock Out. You are taken to Transaction History.

3.2 Transfer (between departments)

  1. Pick the Product, the From Department (source) and the To Department (destination).
  2. The Available in Source quantity is shown. Enter the Quantity to move and click Record Transfer.

3.3 Wastage (damaged / expired)

  1. Choose the Reason: Expired, Damaged, Spillage, Pest / Infestation, or Other.
  2. Select the Product and From Department, enter the Quantity, describe it in Notes, and click Record Wastage.

3.4 Transaction History

  • Filter by Product, Type, Department and date range; Clear resets the filters.
  • Print a single receipt with the printer icon, or Export Excel.
  • To cancel a wrong entry, click Void, type the Reason for Voiding *, and confirm. Voiding restores the stock. (Requires delete permission.)
Module 4

Inventory

See exactly how much of each item you have, and trace every movement.

Who uses it: Store keeper, managers. What you can do: view only.

4.1 Current Stock

  • Shows live quantities grouped by department. Search a product, filter by department, or tick Low stock only.
  • Status is Out of Stock (zero), Low Stock (at or below minimum), or OK.
  • Use Refresh for the latest, or Export Excel.

4.2 Stock Ledger

  1. Select a Product * and a Department * (both required), optionally a date range.
  2. Click View Ledger to see every In, Out, Transfer and Wastage with a running balance, opening and closing rows.
Use this to settle disputes. The ledger shows who did what and when, so you can trace any difference between the book and the shelf.
Module 5

Supplier Payments

Pay suppliers against their invoices, and have trustees approve those payments.

Who uses it: Store manager (records) and trustees (approve). Needs permission: payments — write to record, approve + "Can Approve Payments" flag to approve.

5.1 Recording a payment

  1. Optionally start From Template (a saved supplier + mode combination).
  2. Choose the Supplier * and Payment Date *.
  3. Pick the Payment Mode * (Cash, UPI, Online, NEFT, RTGS, Cheque). For non-cash modes, fill the Cheque No. * or Reference / UTR No. *.
  4. Allocate the payment: click an unpaid invoice to add it, or choose Advance / No Invoice. For each line set how much you are Paying Now (₹) *.
  5. Check the Total Payment Amount and click Submit for Approval.
  6. You may then Save Template for next time, or Skip.
Duplicate guard: If a very similar payment already exists, an amber warning lists the matches. Review carefully — only click Submit Anyway if it is genuinely a separate payment.

5.2 Approving payments

  • Use the tabs All / Pending / Approved / Rejected.
  • Approve: click the green tick → add an optional note → Approve.
  • Reject: click the red cross → type a required Reason * → Reject.
  • Bulk approve: tick several rows → Approve N Selected.
  • Void an approved payment with a reason if it was made in error.
Only trustees can approve. Approve / Reject / Void buttons appear only for users who have the "Can Approve Payments" flag turned on by the administrator.

5.3 Other payment screens

ScreenWhat it shows
Invoice RegisterEvery purchase invoice with its payment status (Unpaid / Partial / Paid). Expand a row for items and payment history.
Upcoming DuesInvoices due soon or overdue. Pick "next 7/15/30/60 days" and click Pay Now → on any row.
TemplatesSaved payment setups you can reuse with one click.
Module 6

Expenses & Budgets

Record day-to-day expenses (salaries, electricity, repairs) and set monthly budgets.

Who uses it: Office staff (record), trustees (approve), admin (budgets). Needs permission: payments — write to record, approve to set budgets.

6.1 Recording an expense

  1. Choose the Category and Expense Date.
  2. If the category is Salary, pick the Staff Member — the payee, amount and description fill in automatically.
  3. Enter the Description, Amount (₹), Payee, and Payment Mode. For Cheque add the Cheque Number; for UPI add the UTR / Reference.
  4. Optionally Attach Receipt (photo or PDF, up to 5 MB).
  5. Click Submit for Approval.

6.2 Expense list & budgets

  • All Expenses — filter by date, category and status; Export PDF. Click an entry number to open its detail.
  • Set Budgets — choose the month/year, optionally Copy last month, type the rupee limit per category, then Save. These limits feed the Monthly P&L report.
Approved only. Budgets and the P&L count only approved expenses — pending ones are not included until a trustee approves them.
Module 7

Mahaprasad

Issue prasad coupons at the counter, collect money, and redeem coupons at the serving point.

Who uses it: Counter / kitchen staff. Needs permission: mahaprasad — issue and redeem.

7.1 Issuing coupons (the counter)

  1. At the start of the day, click Set Opening Float and enter the cash in the drawer (so change is calculated correctly).
  2. Set the number of Persons using the − / + buttons or the preset numbers.
  3. Choose Paid or Free Seva (for free, you may pick an Occasion).
  4. For Paid: choose Cash or UPI. For cash, tap what the customer pays with — the change is shown automatically.
  5. Click Issue Coupon (or just press Enter). The coupon prints if a printer is set up.
Speed tips: Press Enter to issue, and use the number keys for quick amounts. Reprint Last re-prints the previous coupon. My Shift shows your running total and a slip you can Copy for WhatsApp.
Rules: You cannot issue for a past date. When the day reaches the daily cap, issuing stops. If no price is set for the day, coupons are issued as Free. The counter keeps working even if the internet drops — coupons are stored and synced when you are back online.

7.2 Redeeming coupons

  1. Choose Scan QR (redeems automatically when scanned) or Manual Entry.
  2. For manual, type the Coupon Number (e.g. MP-20260611-001) and click Look Up.
  3. Check the details, then click Mark as Redeemed. The screen resets for the next person.
A coupon cannot be redeemed twice. The screen clearly shows if a coupon is Not Found, Expired, or Already Redeemed.

7.3 History, Report & Occasions

ScreenUse

ReportDaily / Monthly / Staff / Wastage analytics. The Daily tab has Copy for WhatsApp for sharing the day's summary.

Module 8

Donations

Record cash and in-kind donations, print receipts, and issue 80G receipts.

Who uses it: Office / donation desk. Needs permission: donations — write.

8.1 Recording a donation

  1. Choose the Donation Type: Named Donor, Hundi (collection box), or Anonymous.
  2. Set the Date * (cannot be in the future) and optional Occasion / Purpose.
  3. For a Named Donor, select an existing donor or type the name and phone. If the phone matches a past donor, click Use Details. Add a PAN Number to enable the 80G Eligible toggle.
  4. For a cash donation, enter the Amount (₹), Payment Mode and reference.
  5. For a kind donation, click Add Item and enter the item, quantity, estimated value and destination department.
  6. Click Record Donation, then Print Receipt if needed.
You must enter either a cash amount or at least one kind item. Kind donations can flow straight into stock (default: Main Store).

8.2 History & receipts

  • Filter by donor/receipt number, date, and type (Named / Hundi / Anonymous).
  • Click a row for the full detail, where you can Print, download a Receipt PDF, or — for eligible named donations — an 80G Receipt.
  • Use Void with a reason to cancel a wrong entry; Export to Excel or PDF.
Module 9

Assets

Track temple items (utensils, mandap, furniture) and manage who has borrowed them.

Who uses it: Store keeper, asset in-charge. Needs permission: assets — write to manage assets, manage to handle returns/reminders.

9.1 The asset list

  • Shows every asset with code, category, total quantity, fine-per-day and whether it is borrowable.
  • Use Add Asset to create one: enter the Asset Name, Category, Total Quantity, Fine Per Day (₹), and tick Available for Borrowing.

9.2 Lending an asset (Borrow Request)

  1. Choose the Borrower Type: a Staff Member or an External Person (for external, capture name, phone and ID proof).
  2. Set the Expected Return Date (limited to the maximum borrow days set in Settings).
  3. Choose the Approved By (Trustee).
  4. Add one or more assets with quantities (availability is shown live), then click Create Borrow Request.

9.3 Returns & reminders

  • Upcoming Returns groups borrows into Overdue, Due Today, Due in 3 Days and Upcoming. Click Return on a card, or Remind All Overdue to message everyone late.
  • On return, set the Actual Return Date and Condition (Good / Fair / Damaged). If late, a fine is calculated automatically — you can Apply or Waive it with a reason.
  • Reports show utilization and fine collection, exportable to Excel/PDF.

Module 11

Masters (setup data)

The reference lists everything else depends on: products, departments, suppliers, categories, units and occasions. Set these up first.

Who uses it: Administrator / manager. Needs permission: masters — write.

11.1 Products

  1. Enter the Product Name, optional Aliases (Hindi/Marathi names), and choose a Category and Unit (all three are required).
  2. Optionally set Min Stock Level, Reorder Point and Standard Rate (₹), and tick properties like Puja / Ritual Item or Perishable Item.
  3. Click Create Product.
Set Min Stock Level for important items — this is what drives the Low Stock alerts and the red warnings on the Stock Out screen.

Module 12

Administration

Manage staff accounts, what each role can do, the activity log, and system settings.

Who uses it: Administrator / super admin only.

12.1 Users

  1. Fill the Full Name, Email Address, optional Mobile Number, and a Password (at least 6 characters; only needed when creating).
  2. Assign a Role. For non-admin roles, tick the Department Access the user is allowed to work in.
  3. Optionally set Monthly Salary, and turn on Can Approve Asset Requests and/or Can Approve Payments for trustees.
  4. Click Create User.
"Can Approve Payments" is the trustee power. Only turn it on for people authorised to approve money going out.

Appendix

Roles & Permissions

The system controls what each person can see and do through their role. If a menu or button is missing, it is because your role does not include that permission — ask the administrator.

How it works

  • Each user has one role (e.g. Store Manager, Kitchen Staff, Trustee, Viewer).
  • A role is a set of permissions grouped by area: Masters, Transactions, Reports, Assets, Donations, Payments, Mahaprasad, Users.
  • Each area can allow read (view), write/create (add & edit), delete, approve, or manage.
  • Two special powers are set per user, not per role: Can Approve PaymentsCan Approve Asset Requests
  • Non-admin users can also be limited to certain departments.
