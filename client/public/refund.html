Cancellation & Refund Policy

Shri Mangal Grah Mandir, Amalner · managed by Mangalgrah Seva Sanstha, Amalner

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy covers Mahaprasad coupons bought online on this website. Mahaprasad is prepared for the number of coupons sold for each day, so a coupon is for its day only.

Cancellation

  • A coupon cannot be cancelled once it has been paid for, and it cannot be moved to another day.
  • A coupon not used on its day expires and is not refunded.
  • Before you pay, you can simply close the payment window: nothing is charged and no coupon is issued.

When we refund

  • Paid, but no coupon issued — for example, the day sold out while your payment was being made, or the payment arrived after the day was over. The full amount is refunded automatically; you do not need to ask.
  • Charged twice for the same order, or money taken without a coupon for any other reason. Tell us (see below) and we refund the extra amount.
  • The temple cannot serve Mahaprasad on the day of your coupon. We refund the coupon in full.

How refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to the same account, card or UPI ID you paid from, through Razorpay. We do not refund in cash.
  • Automatic refunds are started at once. Others are started within 7 working days of your request.
  • Your bank usually credits the money within 5–7 working days after the refund is started.
