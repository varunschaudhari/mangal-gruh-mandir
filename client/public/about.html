About the temple

Shri Mangal Grah Mandir in Amalner (District Jalgaon, Maharashtra) is a temple of Lord Mangal (Mars). Devotees come for abhishek and puja, Mahaprasad and darshan. The temple is managed by Mangalgrah Seva Sanstha, Amalner, a registered public trust.

Services

Booking receipts and coupon details are sent to the devotee's mobile number on WhatsApp or SMS. Each receipt has a QR code that is scanned at the hall entrance.
