Privacy Policy

Shri Mangal Grah Mandir, Amalner · managed by Mangalgrah Seva Sanstha, Amalner

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Who we are

Mangalgrah Seva Sanstha, Amalner (Public Trust Reg. No. F-13350/Jalgaon) runs Shri Mangal Grah Mandir at Chopda Road, Amalner, Dist. Jalgaon, Maharashtra 425401. The trust decides how devotees' information is used. The temple's booking and office software is provided by Ashaparth AI, which handles this information only on the trust's behalf and instructions.

What we collect

  • Pooja bookings: name, mobile number, the seva, date and batch; and, if you give them for the sankalp or for prasad sent by post, family names, gotra, rashi / nakshatra and postal address.
  • Payments: amount, payment mode and payment reference (for UPI or card). We do not store card numbers or UPI PINs.
  • Donations: name, mobile number, amount or items donated, and PAN when you ask for an 80G receipt.
  • Mahaprasad coupons: mobile number and the coupons issued; when bought online, also the name you give (optional) and the payment details from Razorpay (payment ID, method such as UPI or card, amount and time).
  • Hall entry: the time your receipt's QR code is scanned at the hall.

Why we use it

  • To book and perform the seva, read the sankalp, and manage entry to the hall.
  • To send your receipt, booking details and coupon numbers to your mobile number on WhatsApp or SMS.
  • To issue donation and 80G receipts, and to keep the accounts the law requires of a public trust.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Message services: WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) and our SMS provider receive your mobile number and the message text, only to deliver it to you.
  • Payment gateway: online payments are made on Razorpay Software Private Limited's payment page. Razorpay receives your mobile number and the amount from us, and the card, UPI or bank details you enter there, to process the payment under its own privacy policy. We never receive your card number, UPI PIN or bank password.
  • Our software provider (Ashaparth AI) and its hosting services, which keep the data on the trust's behalf.
  • Government authorities when the law requires it, for example donation details for income tax (80G) reporting.

How long we keep it

Booking and donation records are kept as long as Indian accounting and tax laws require public trusts to keep them. Other information is removed when it is no longer needed.

How we protect it

Access is limited to authorised temple staff with their own sign-in, every change is recorded in an audit log, data is sent over encrypted connections, and the receipt links sent to you are private and hide most of your mobile number.

WhatsApp and SMS messages

We send only messages about your booking, donation or coupons, not promotions. If you do not want these messages, tell the temple office or write to us (see below), and we will stop sending them.

Your rights and deleting your data

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted.

To ask for your data to be deleted:
  • Email mangalgrahamandir@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request", your name and the mobile number you used, or
  • call 02587-299999, or speak to the temple office.

We will confirm within 30 days. Records the law requires us to keep (such as issued receipts and the accounts) are kept for the required period and then deleted.
