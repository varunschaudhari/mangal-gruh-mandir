Mahaprasad coupon

One coupon is one plate of Mahaprasad, served at the temple's Mahaprasad hall on the day chosen. The price depends on the day of the week:

DayPrice per plate
The current prices are shown on the online coupon page before you pay.
  • Prices are in Indian Rupees (₹) and are the full amount you pay. No booking fee or other charge is added at payment.
  • The total (price per plate × number of plates) is shown before you pay, and on the payment receipt.
  • The same prices apply at the temple counter.
  • Prices may change from time to time; a coupon already bought keeps the price paid.

How you pay

Online payments are made through Razorpay, a payment gateway regulated in India, by UPI, debit or credit card, net banking or wallet. At the temple counter you can pay in cash or by UPI.
