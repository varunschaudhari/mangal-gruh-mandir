2. What you buy

A Shri Mangal Mahaprasad coupon gives one person one plate of Shri Mangal Mahaprasad at the temple's Shri Mangal Mahaprasad hall on the day you choose. When you buy more than one plate in one order, you get one coupon (one QR code) for that many persons, to be used together.

3. Ordering and payment

  1. Choose the day, the number of plates and your mobile number. The price per plate and the total are shown before you pay (see Pricing).
  2. You pay through Razorpay by UPI, card, net banking or wallet. We do not see or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank password.
  3. Your order is confirmed only when the payment succeeds. You then see your coupon on the screen, and it is sent to your mobile number on WhatsApp or SMS. The coupon page also has your payment receipt.
  4. Coupons for a day are limited. A day may close for online orders when it is full or at the time set by the temple; the website shows which days are open.

4. Using the coupon

  • Show the coupon's QR code (on your phone, or printed) at the Shri Mangal Mahaprasad hall on the chosen day, during the temple's Shri Mangal Mahaprasad serving hours.
  • Each coupon can be used once. A group coupon is for all its persons at the same time.
  • The coupon link works like a paper coupon: anyone who has it can use it, so do not share it with others.
  • A coupon not used on its day expires.

5. Cancellations and refunds

Coupons cannot be cancelled, refunded or moved to another day once bought, except as described in the Cancellation & Refund Policy (for example, when your payment went through but no coupon could be issued).

6. Changes and availability

The temple may change serving hours, prices for future orders, or stop online orders for a time, for festivals, maintenance or other reasons. A coupon already bought keeps its day and price. If the temple cannot serve Shri Mangal Mahaprasad on a day for which you hold a coupon, we refund it as set out in the refund policy.
